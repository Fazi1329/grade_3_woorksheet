# Grade 3 Islamic Studies: Zakat Interactive Online Quiz Challenge (20 Marks)

A fun, colourful, fully interactive online educational quiz designed for Grade 3 students based strictly on Chapter 5: *"Zakah – The Purifying Dues"* (الزَّكَاة, Pages 21–22).

---

## 🎮 Play the Quiz

- **Online Quiz Homepage:** [`index.html`](index.html) or [`quiz.html`](quiz.html)
- **Teacher's Answer Key & Lesson Guide:** [`quiz_answer_key.html`](quiz_answer_key.html)

---

## 🌟 Interactive Quiz Features

- **Personalized Student Experience**: Enter the student's name on the cheerful welcome screen to personalize all greetings and generate an achievement certificate.
- **Audio & Animations**: Synthesized sound effects (Web Audio API) for clicks, correct answers, encouraging cues, and a grand victory fanfare.
- **Full-Screen Canvas Confetti**: Celebratory confetti upon completing the challenge!
- **Instant Feedback**: Every question displays immediate explanations and verified textbook quotes.
- **Dynamic Scoring**: Automatically tracks marks in real-time up to exactly 20 marks.
- **Printable Certificate of Achievement**: Generates a star certificate with the child's name, topic, score, and percentage.
- **Question Review Mode**: Detailed review of all 20 questions with student answers and verified textbook citations.

## 📋 Quiz Mark Allocation (Exactly 20 Marks)

| Section | Activity Type | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|:---:|
| **Section A** | Underline / Select the Correct Answer | Q1 – Q5 | **5 Marks** |
| **Section B** | Choose the Correct Answer (Large Buttons) | Q6 – Q10 | **5 Marks** |
| **Section C** | Interactive Drag-and-Drop / Click Matching | Q11 – Q15 | **5 Marks** |
| **Section D** | Fill in the Missing Word (Word Bank Chips) | Q16 – Q20 | **5 Marks** |
| **Total** | **Strictly verified against textbook pages 21–22** | **20 Questions** | **20 Marks** |

## 🏆 Scoring Rubric & Feedback

- **18–20 marks:** *"🌟 Excellent! You are a Zakat Star!"*
- **15–17 marks:** *"👏 Great work! You understand the lesson well."*
- **10–14 marks:** *"📚 Good effort! Practise a little more."*
- **0–9 marks:** *"💪 Keep learning! Try again and improve your score."*

## 🌐 Live Online Access (GitHub Pages)

To make the quiz playable online from anywhere:
1. Go to **[GitHub Repository Settings > Pages](https://github.com/Fazi1329/grade_3_woorksheet/settings/pages)**.
2. Under **Build and deployment > Branch**, choose **`main`** and folder **`/ (root)`**.
3. Click **Save**.
4. The live quiz is served at: **`https://fazi1329.github.io/grade_3_woorksheet/`**
